01 / The idea

AI should change how you approach a problem, not how fast you fix it.

AI can be an active thinking partner, one that helps you examine assumptions, investigate unfamiliar territory, make connections, and see possibilities you might otherwise miss.

Collaborating with AI doesn't mean surrendering your judgment or critical thought. You bring the context, experience, curiosity, and responsibility. AI contributes questions, perspectives, and possibilities worth examining. The thinking happens together; the decisions remain yours.

02 / The approach

Start with a real problem.

Not a list of prompts to memorize or a polished demo with an obvious answer. A real-life use case with uncertainty and complexity.

01 — Recognize

Find the opportunity.

Identify a meaningful challenge and ask where working with AI might change your approach.

02 — Investigate

Think it through together.

Share context. Question assumptions. Look for missing information. Explore and test possible paths.

03 — Decide

Use your judgment.

Evaluate what AI offers, challenge weak reasoning, and make decisions you can explain and defend.

03 / Who it's for

For community builders and managers.

Especially when the systems behind those communities are anything but straightforward.

Primary focus

Working across disconnected tools?

If your community runs through Slack, Discord, spreadsheets, or a collection of makeshift workflows, you know how much manual effort and ambiguity can hide between systems.

Also welcome

Working inside a dedicated platform?

Even with systems such as Higher Logic, Circle, or Gainsight, making sense of information, designing workflows, and solving unfamiliar problems still demand human judgment.
